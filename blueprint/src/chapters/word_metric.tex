\chapter{Word metrics}
\label{chap:word-metric}

Milestone M3. $P$ is a generating family $\iota \to G$ of a group $G$, and $|g|_P$ the word
length of $g$ with respect to $P$. The generating families, the word length, the word metric and
the Cayley graph are copies of open Mathlib pull requests (see \texttt{ATTRIBUTION.md}); the
copy $(G, d_P)$ of $G$ with the word metric is ours. Their elementary API (subadditivity, inverses,
word length zero, generators having length at most one) is used in the proofs below without
being a node.
