\chapter{Coarse spaces}
\label{chap:coarse-spaces}

Milestone M5. The project contains a copy of S.~Ghobash's coarse-space library (see
\texttt{ATTRIBUTION.md}): coarse spaces, coarsely close maps, coarse maps, coarse equivalences and
the metric coarse structure of a pseudometric space, whose controlled sets are the sets of pairs at
bounded distance. It proves a \v{S}varc--Milnor statement as a coarse equivalence, without
constants, word metric or finite generation. Nothing yet connects it to the metric notions of the
other chapters, except that Chapter~\ref{chap:bounded-geometry} uses its metric coarse structure
for bounded geometry.
